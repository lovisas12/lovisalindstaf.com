Hello!

I'm Lovisa, a multidisciplinary designer who likes being where things are still taking shape.

My foundation is UX. Over the years the work has grown outward into product and visual design, brand, startups and technology, communication, events and the people who make things happen.

What ties it together is the moment before anything is defined. A rough idea, a few conversations, a lot of possibility. I like turning that into something people can see, understand and want to be part of, then staying close enough to see it launch.

I do my best work with others: founders with conviction, teams who care about taste, stakeholders who need a clear picture. Strategy and aesthetics, words and interfaces: to me, it's the same conversation.
